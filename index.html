About VIX

A diversified holding company built for Africa's next growth era.

VIX Investment Limited is a diversified investment and infrastructure company developing a portfolio of operating businesses across energy, healthcare, real estate, technology, logistics, and essential services.

VIX Energy is the leading active subsidiary, delivering practical solar power, inverter systems, CCTV security, smart homes, and electrical engineering solutions that help customers reduce downtime and build resilient operations.

Across the group, the company focuses on sustainable development, smart technology, renewable energy, and infrastructure growth with a premium corporate standard.
